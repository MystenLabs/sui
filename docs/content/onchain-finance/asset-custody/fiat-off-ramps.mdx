Fiat off-ramp providers accept Sui-native tokens and send fiat currency to a user's bank account, card, or payment method. You embed an off-ramp provider into your app so users can convert their Sui assets to traditional currency without leaving your interface.

Off-ramp coverage on Sui is more limited than on-ramp coverage. Fewer providers support Sui off-ramps, regional availability is narrower, and certain assets (notably USDC) have minimal off-ramp support. Verify current provider capabilities before integrating.

## Asset limitations

When you choose off-ramp providers, be aware of the following limitations:

- **USDC off-ramp on Sui:** USDC off-ramp coverage on Sui is limited. The Onramper audit (January 2026) found zero working USDC off-ramp quotes across 28 tested countries. Meld lists USDC as supported through its aggregated providers, but verify current availability before relying on it. If your users need to off-ramp USDC, confirm provider support directly.
- **SUI sell limits:** Off-ramp providers that use MoonPay as the underlying provider enforce sell limits (approximately 14 SUI minimum and 250 SUI maximum per transaction).
- **KYC requirements:** All off-ramp flows require Know Your Customer (KYC) verification. Factor this into your user experience design because first-time users need to complete identity verification before they can convert assets to fiat.

:::info

Off-ramp coverage is evolving. Provider asset support and regional availability change frequently. Confirm current capabilities with your chosen provider before launching an integration.

:::

## Choosing a provider

Select an off-ramp provider based on 4 criteria:

1. **Regional coverage:** Match the provider's supported countries and payment methods to where your users are located. Off-ramp regional coverage is significantly narrower than on-ramp coverage, especially in Asia-Pacific.
2. **Asset support:** Verify that the provider supports off-ramping the specific Sui assets your users hold. SUI has the broadest off-ramp support. USDC off-ramp support is limited.
3. **Payment methods:** Users in different regions expect different payout methods (bank transfer, card, PayPal, PIX). Match the provider's methods to your users' expectations.
4. **Sell limits and KYC:** Understand the per-transaction limits and KYC requirements each provider enforces. These affect how much your users can convert and how quickly they can complete their first transaction.

## Security considerations

When you integrate a fiat off-ramp into your app, follow these practices:

- **Verify transaction intent before signing.** Display clear, human-readable descriptions of what each off-ramp transaction does before the user approves it. Show the asset amount, destination fiat currency, and payout method. Avoid blind signing of opaque transaction bytes ([Security Best Practices](https://docs.sui.io/develop/security/best-practices)).
- **Validate provider package IDs.** If a ramp provider uses onchain smart contracts for settlement, verify their package addresses at runtime. Do not trust contract addresses based on names alone ([Security Best Practices](https://docs.sui.io/develop/security/best-practices)).
- **Separate keys by network.** Use different wallet addresses for Testnet and Mainnet off-ramp integrations. Never reuse Testnet keys for production transactions ([Security Best Practices](https://docs.sui.io/develop/security/best-practices)).
- **Validate settlement confirmations.** Verify signatures and check data freshness on all webhook callbacks and settlement confirmations before updating your app state. Define explicit failure behavior for missing, stale, or malformed confirmation data ([Security Best Practices](https://docs.sui.io/develop/security/best-practices)).
