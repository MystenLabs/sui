Fiat on-ramp providers accept traditional currency payments (credit cards, bank transfers, mobile wallets) and deliver Sui-native tokens to a user's wallet address. You embed an on-ramp provider into your app so users can purchase Sui assets without leaving your interface.

## Provider comparison

The following table summarizes providers with live Sui on-ramp integration as of August 2026. All columns refer to Sui chain and Sui asset support specifically.

| **Provider** | **Type** | **Supported assets** | **Documentation** |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Banxa](https://banxa.com/) | Direct | SUI, USDC | [Banxa docs](https://docs.banxa.com/products/hosted-checkout/docs/reference/supported-cryptocurrencies-and-blockchains) |
| [Bridge.xyz](https://bridge.xyz) | Direct | USDSui | [Bridge docs](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/platform/issuance/designing-your-stablecoin#supported-chains) |
| [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) | Direct | SUI, USDSui | [Stripe crypto on-ramp docs](https://docs.stripe.com/crypto/onramp) |
| [Coinbase](https://coinbase.com/) | Direct | SUI, USDC, WAL, DEEP | [Coinbase CDP docs](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/onramp/offramp/offramp-overview) |
| [Alchemy Pay](https://alchemypay.org/) | Direct | AUSD, SUI, USD | [Alchemy Pay docs](https://alchemypay.readme.io/docs/api-integration-3) |
| [OnRamp.money](https://onramp.money/) | Direct | SUI, USDC, WAL | [OnRamp.money docs](https://onramp.money/supported-token/) |
| [CoinVoyage](https://www.coinvoyage.io/) | Direct | Sui Dollar | [CoinVoyage docs](https://docs.coinvoyage.io/sdk/paykit-headless#sui-payment-signatures) |
| [Onramper](https://onramper.com/) | Aggregator | SUI | [Onramper docs](https://docs.onramper.com/docs/network-support) |
| [Meld](https://www.meld.io) | Aggregator | SUI, USDC, CETUS, NAVI | [Meld docs](https://www.meld.io/coverage/service-providers?cryptoCurrencies=SUI) |

On-ramp providers fall into 2 categories:

- **Direct providers:** Banxa, Unlimit, Bridge.xyz, Stripe, Coinbase, Alchemy Pay, OnRamp.money, CoinVoyage, and Bungee connect directly to payment rails and process transactions themselves. You integrate their SDK or API and manage the provider relationship.

- **Aggregators:** Onramper and Meld route transactions through multiple underlying providers (for example, Onramper routes through Kraken, Topper, Banxa, and MoonPay for Sui on-ramps). Aggregators simplify integration because a single SDK covers multiple providers and regions.

:::info

Aggregators like Meld and Onramper route transactions through multiple underlying providers. This broadens regional and asset coverage but means your actual payment experience depends on which sub-provider handles a given transaction.

:::
