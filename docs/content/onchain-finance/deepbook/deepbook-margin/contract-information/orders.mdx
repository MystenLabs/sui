The `pool_proxy` module provides wrapper functions for trading with margin managers. These functions enable placing orders, modifying and canceling them, and managing staking and governance participation through a margin manager. All trading operations require the margin manager to be associated with the correct DeepBook pool.

## How orders work with margin managers

A margin manager acts as the on-behalf-of account for all order operations in a DeepBook margin pool. When you place an order, the margin manager supplies the required collateral from its balance manager. The pool records the order against that margin manager's account.

Orders fall into two categories based on when margin trading is enabled:

- **Standard orders** (limit and market): Available when margin trading is enabled for the pool. These orders can open new positions or increase existing debt.
- **Reduce-only orders**: Available at any time, including when margin trading is disabled. These orders can only decrease an existing debt position. Use reduce-only orders to close positions without increasing exposure.

After an order fills, the settled amounts stay in the pool until you explicitly withdraw them back to the margin manager's balance manager.

## What types of orders can you place?

You can place limit orders, market orders, and reduce-only variants of each. Limit orders specify a price and quantity. Market orders fill immediately at the best available price. Reduce-only orders enforce that the resulting position is smaller than the current debt, preventing accidental position increases.

## How do you modify or cancel an order?

You can modify an existing open order to change its quantity. To cancel, you can target a single order by ID, provide a list of order IDs, or cancel all open orders for the margin manager in a single call.

## How do you withdraw funds after a trade?

After an order fills, call `withdraw_settled_amounts` to move the proceeds from the pool back to the margin manager's balance manager. Settled amounts are not automatically returned.
