The `pool_proxy` module provides wrapper functions for trading with margin managers. These functions enable placing orders, modifying and canceling them, and managing staking and governance participation through a margin manager. All trading operations require the margin manager to be associated with the correct DeepBook pool.

## What are margin orders in DeepBook?

Margin orders in DeepBook are trading instructions placed through a margin manager rather than a standard balance manager. The margin manager holds your collateral and tracks your debt, and the `pool_proxy` module routes orders through it to the underlying DeepBook pool.

There are two primary order types:

- **Limit orders** execute at a specified price or better. The order rests in the order book until it fills or you cancel it.
- **Market orders** execute immediately at the best available prices. They do not rest in the order book.

Regular limit and market orders require margin trading to be enabled for the pool, and abort with `EPoolNotEnabledForMarginTrading` otherwise. When the margin manager carries debt, the order must also leave the post-trade risk ratio at or above the pool's minimum open risk ratio.

**Reduce-only orders** are a variant of both types. Reduce-only is a direction guard rather than a size limit: a bid requires base (short-side) debt, and an ask requires quote (long-side) debt and can sell up to the base the manager holds. The order must also leave the post-trade risk ratio no worse than it was before the trade. Reduce-only orders skip the margin-trading-enabled check, so you can still wind a position down after an admin disables margin trading for the pool.

Placing an order never borrows. Debt changes only through the `borrow_*` and `repay_*` functions on the margin manager. To close a position and repay in the same call, use `place_reduce_only_market_order_and_repay_loan` or `place_reduce_only_limit_order_and_repay_loan`.

## How do I place an order using a margin manager?

Call `place_limit_order_v2` or `place_market_order_v2` in the `pool_proxy_upgraded` module, passing the margin registry, your margin manager, the DeepBook pool, both margin pools, and the base and quote Pyth `PriceInfoObject`s. The margin manager checks ownership against the transaction sender, so there is no capability object to pass.

The following example shows how to call `place_limit_order_v2` in a Move transaction:

```move
use deepbook_margin::pool_proxy_upgraded;

// Place a limit order through a margin manager
pool_proxy_upgraded::place_limit_order_v2(
    registry,
    margin_manager,
    pool,
    base_margin_pool,
    quote_margin_pool,
    base_oracle,
    quote_oracle,
    client_order_id,
    order_type,
    self_matching_option,
    price,
    quantity,
    is_bid,
    pay_with_deep,
    expire_timestamp,
    clock,
    ctx,
);
```

To place a reduce-only order, call `place_reduce_only_limit_order_v2` or `place_reduce_only_market_order_v2` instead. Both take the same parameters and apply the reduce-only direction guard.

:::info

The original `place_limit_order`, `place_market_order`, and `place_reduce_only_*` entries in `pool_proxy`, along with their legacy-Pyth `_v2` twins in the same module, all abort. Call the `_v2` functions in `pool_proxy_upgraded` instead.

:::

## How do I modify or cancel an order?

Use `modify_order` to reduce the quantity of a resting limit order. The new quantity must be lower than the original, otherwise the call aborts with `ENewQuantityMustBeLessThanOriginal`. Provide the order ID returned when you placed the order.

To cancel orders, use one of three functions depending on scope:

- `cancel_order` cancels a single order by ID.
- `cancel_orders` cancels a list of orders by ID.
- `cancel_all_orders` cancels every open order for the margin manager in the pool.

## How do I withdraw funds after a trade?

Settled amounts flow back into the margin manager's balance manager on the manager's next order, modify, or cancel in that pool. Call `withdraw_settled_amounts` to settle without placing another order. Anyone can call `withdraw_settled_amounts_permissionless` to settle on behalf of a margin manager they do not own.
