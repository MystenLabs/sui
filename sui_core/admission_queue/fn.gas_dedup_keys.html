pub fn gas_dedup_keys(transactions: &[ConsensusTransaction]) -> Vec<DedupKey> ⓘ
Expand description

Dedup keys of the gas payment objects of the user transactions in transactions, sorted with duplicates removed. Coin reservation refs are excluded: they name a reusable address balance rather than an exclusive object, so distinct transactions may share one. A soft bundle is one entry keyed on all its members’ gas, so it is refused or displaced whole.
