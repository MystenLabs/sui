Expand description

Resolves once every pipeline in pipelines is caught up according to the module-level rule.

local is this process’s durable watermarks, startup the stored watermark rows read at startup, and peers what this instance has heard from its peers. Re-evaluates whenever local or peers changes, and when the peer wait ends. If the peers sender is dropped, the latest peer watermarks still count.

If the local sender is dropped, the store and therefore the indexer have stopped, so the process is shutting down; the future then never resolves and readiness is never reported.
