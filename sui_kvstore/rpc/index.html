The kvstore gRPC server.

It hosts grpc.health.v1 and gRPC reflection, and further services, such as watermark_service::WatermarkService, are registered through RpcService::add_service. The overall server status (the “” service name) and every registered service report NOT_SERVING until the readiness future passed to RpcService::run resolves, and SERVING from then on. Readiness gates the instance once, after startup: the status latches, so lag that develops later does not withdraw it.
