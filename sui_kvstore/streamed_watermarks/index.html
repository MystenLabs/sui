Module streamed_watermarks 

Source
Expand description

Follows the WatermarkService streams of kvstore instances and merges their durable watermarks. A kvstore instance follows its peers to compare its own progress against them (crate::readiness), and a reader follows the instances that write the data it serves to learn how far that data goes.
