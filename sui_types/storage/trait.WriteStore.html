fn insert_checkpoint_contents_digests( &self, checkpoint: &VerifiedCheckpoint, contents: CheckpointContents, ) -> Result<()>

Records the contents of a checkpoint without its full contents. The caller must have inserted every transaction and effects of the checkpoint first, because readers treat stored contents as proof that they are available.
